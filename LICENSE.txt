Icons / Иконки — game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0)

Every Word, Gift, Theurgy Art and Low Magic tradition in this system's compendia
uses an icon from the game-icons.net collection (https://game-icons.net,
https://github.com/game-icons/icons). The source SVGs were recoloured to a
per-Word palette and rendered to webp; greater gifts additionally get a gold
medallion frame. The results live in:

  assets/icons/*.webp        — Words, lesser gifts, Arts
  assets/gift-frames/*.webp  — greater gifts (`system.greater === true`)
